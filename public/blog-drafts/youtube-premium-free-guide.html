Method 1: The Official Free Trial (Up to 2 Months Free)

The simplest legit method. Many accounts in India still see a trial offer inside the YouTube app:

  • Open the YouTube app and tap your profile → Premium
  • If you see "Try it free", tap it and add a payment method (you aren't charged during the trial)
  • Set a reminder 2–3 days before it ends so you can cancel before auto-billing kicks in

Eligibility varies by account — new accounts get the best offers. You can cancel anytime from YouTube's Paid memberships page.
