Method 1: The Official Free Trial

Spotify regularly offers 2–3 months of Premium free to new (and sometimes returning) users:

  • Open Spotify → Premium tab and look for a trial banner
  • Offers change through the year — festive seasons usually bring the longest trials
  • Add a payment method to start; cancel before it ends and you pay nothing

Already used a trial on your main account? A fresh account often becomes eligible again — just know that your playlists won't carry over unless you transfer them.

Method 2: Student Discount (Half Price)

Verified students get Premium at ₹59/month — half the individual price. Verification runs through Spotify's student-check partner once a year. If you qualify, this is the cheapest official route by far.

Method 3: Split a Family or Duo Plan

The Family plan at ₹179 covers 6 people — about ₹30 per person per month. Spotify requires members to share the same home address, so this works best with family or housemates. The Duo plan (₹149 for 2) is the fallback if you can't fill six slots.

Method 4: Telecom, Bank & Brand Offers

Keep an eye on these — they rotate through the year:

  • Mobile plans: Jio, Airtel and Vi periodically bundle music subscriptions with prepaid/postpaid recharges — check the benefits section of your operator's app.
  • Bank & card offers: credit/debit card reward programs sometimes include Spotify trial codes.
  • Device bundles: new phone and audio-brand launches occasionally include 3–6 months of Premium.

Method 5: Spotify's Own Promotions

Spotify runs limited-time deals directly — discounted first months, "3 months for the price of 1" style offers, and win-back deals for cancelled subscribers. If you cancelled before, check your email: lapsed users regularly get comeback offers.
