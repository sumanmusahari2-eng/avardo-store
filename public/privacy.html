Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the store: your name, email address, order history, and any payment reference you share with us. Your cart is stored in your own browser's local storage.

2. How we use it

Your details are used to process orders, deliver digital products, provide support, and prevent fraud. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Payments

Payments are made manually via UPI directly to us. We never ask for or store your UPI PIN, card numbers, or banking passwords.

4. Cookies

We use a single secure cookie to keep you logged in. No advertising trackers are used.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to correct or delete your account data at any time via WhatsApp support. Order records needed for accounting may be retained as required by law.
